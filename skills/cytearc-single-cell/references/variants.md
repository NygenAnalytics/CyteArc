# Mitochondrial and targeted variants

Import each cell's counts of each allele at variant sites, from mitochondrial lineage tracing (mtscATAC-seq, MAESTER) or targeted genotyping (GoT, GIFT, Tapestri, DAb-seq), join them to the store's cells, check depth, read the counts as tables, select informative mitochondrial variants and call the clones they mark, and call genotypes at targeted sites and the clones their mutant calls define. Docs: <https://docs.nygen.io/CyteArc/api/variants.html>, <https://docs.nygen.io/CyteArc/tutorials/mitochondrial-lineage-tracing>.

## When to use

- Per-cell allele counts from mgatk (`<name>.{A,C,G,T}.txt.gz` with `<contig>_refAllele.txt`), maegatk (the same files, six columns), cellsnp-lite (`cellSNP.tag.{AD,DP,OTH}.mtx`), MQuad (`passed_ad.mtx`, `passed_dp.mtx`), VarTrix's coverage mode (alt and ref matrices, VCF, barcodes), MiTo's allele frequency matrix (`.h5ad` with `AD` and `DP` layers), giftwrap (`counts.N.h5`, or GEO's `probe_reads.tsv.gz` with `barcodes.tsv.gz` and `manifest.tsv`), GoT tables (IronThrone's `summTable.txt`, GEO's per-cell tables, GoT-Multi's `ironthrone_out_pp.csv` and `got_multi_out_refined.csv`, Gotcha's CSVs), Tapestri Pipeline 2.0 `<sample>.dna+protein.h5` files and DAb-seq `.hdf5` files.
- A Tapestri file gets a store of its own: `cytearc.TapestriToZarr(cytearc.TapestriReader(path), zarr_loc=...).dump()` writes the amplicon reads as the `CNV` assay and the antibody reads as `ADT`, with `sample_name` and `filtered` as cell metadata and the file's panel, genome and pipeline version in the store's `tapestri` attribute; open it with `default_assay="ADT"` (or `"CNV"`).
- Not for BAM or FASTQ files: run the tool first. Not for clones that a tool already called (MiTo's `obs['MiTo_clone']`, Numbat): use `ds.clones.import_clones`.

## Key concepts

- `ds.variants.import_counts(paths, *, cell_selection, barcode_prefix=None, barcode_suffix=None, barcode_pattern=None, format=None, count_unit=None, swap_strands=False, include_variants=None, invalidate_cache=False)` saves a datastore-scoped `variant_counts` artifact. Each path is a folder (mgatk, maegatk, cellsnp-lite, MQuad, VarTrix, giftwrap's GEO tables) or a file (giftwrap's HDF5, MiTo's h5ad, a GoT table, a Tapestri `.h5`, a DAb-seq `.hdf5`), and several paths must share a format. `format` is one of `"mgatk"`, `"maegatk"`, `"cellsnp_lite"`, `"mquad"`, `"vartrix"`, `"mito"`, `"giftwrap"`, `"ironthrone"`, `"got"`, `"got_multi"`, `"gotcha"`, `"tapestri"`, `"dab_seq"`.
- Tapestri: the variants that pass the pipeline's filters (`filtered == 0`) plus `include_variants`, named as the file names them (`"chr1:115256513:G/T"`) or by position (`"chr1:115256513"`, every variant there). Each position is one site whose alternatives share its depth `DP`; alternative reads are `AF × DP / 100` (no reference reads, and one position's frequencies may add up to more than 100%); `NGT` is the call column with `GQ` as `quality`, kept only where the cell has reads. Cells are `<sample>__<barcode>`, as `TapestriToZarr` names them. Rows with an empty `ALT` or non-base alleles (`*`, `.`, `C+T`) are skipped with a warning, on reuse too (diagnostics `skippedEmptyAlternatives`, `skippedOtherAlleles`, `skippedVariantExamples`); the source record also keeps the pipeline's `gq_cutoff`, `dp_cutoff` and `af_cutoff`. DAb-seq: every variant, `AD`, `RD` (once per position), `DP`, `GT` (diploid) with `GQ`; -1 is missing; each FLT3-ITD variant is a named site of its own, such as `FLT3-ITD:chr13:28608251:T/TCTC`, with alternative reads only. Rows beyond the memory budget spill to sorted runs in `TMPDIR`, about 4 bytes per mgatk count row.
- Six-column mgatk files (`--emit-base-qualities`) look like maegatk's, which count strands the other way round, so such a folder raises `ValueError` until `format` names it. maegatk's strands are swapped back on import; mgatk `call` or `bcall` output from 0.4.0 to 0.5.8 needs `swap_strands=True`. Strand only means something in DNA libraries (mtscATAC-seq), not RNA (MAESTER).
- cellsnp-lite, MQuad and VarTrix need `count_unit="umis"` or `"reads"`, since their files do not say; other formats count in their own unit (mgatk reads, maegatk molecules, giftwrap and GoT UMIs, Gotcha reads), and a different `count_unit` raises `ValueError`. A MiTo matrix counts molecules when its `uns` holds `molecules` or a `pp_method` of maegatk or ReDeeM; otherwise, such as for mgatk reads that MiTo 1 assembled, it needs `count_unit`.
- Sites: every reference position but `N` (mgatk, maegatk; 16,568 for rCRS), each VCF record (cellsnp-lite, VarTrix), each passed variant (MQuad), each position of the matrix (MiTo), each probe (giftwrap) or target (GoT). A one-target GoT table names its site after the file (`Patient1_TET2.2847_summTable.txt` gives `Patient1_TET2.2847`). GoT-Multi's channels are sites `BCOR (geneseq)` and `BCOR (probebc)`, and its genotypes sit on `BCOR`.
- Alleles have roles: `"reference"` (reference base, wild type), `"alternative"` (other base, mutant, a GIFT manifest's expected gapfill) and `"other"` (cellsnp-lite's other bases, GoT's ambiguous UMIs, gapfills no manifest names).
- Depth 0 means not covered: an allele frequency there is undefined, never 0. The `depth_definition` attribute says whether depth counts every read (`"all"`) or only the reference and alternative (`"reference_and_alternative"`: MQuad, VarTrix, GoT tables without ambiguous calls, Gotcha). cellsnp-lite's depth is DP plus OTH and its reference count DP less AD.
- The join: a cell's ID is `barcode_prefix + barcode + barcode_suffix`, and `barcode_pattern`'s named group `barcode` keeps part of a barcode. mgatk with `-bt CB` on a Cell Ranger ARC ATAC BAM gives the RNA barcodes with `-1`. Multiplexed Flex barcodes (24 nt) join a singleplex library with `barcode_pattern=r"^(?P<barcode>[ACGT]{16})", barcode_suffix="-1"`. Several libraries take one prefix each.
- Several paths share sites, so they must lie on one reference: a contig of another length or sequence, one sequence under two contig names (mgatk's contig is the name of `<contig>_refAllele.txt` without the base files' prefix, so name copies alike), files that give an allele two roles, or a cell counted twice at a site raise `ValueError`. They must also share the unit, the depth definition and the count fields: import four- and six-column mgatk folders, or cellsnp-lite folders with and without OTH, apart. GoT-Multi's refined table holds calls alone and imports with the stage-1 table.
- The artifact: canonical `mean_depth` (depth summed over sites over the number of sites; NaN for a cell no file names), `n_covered_sites`, `listed`. `ds.select_cells(counts, low=10)` keeps cells with a mean depth above 10. mgatk scores cells by the same bound but averages over every reference position, the `N` at 3107 included, so cells within a few thousandths of 10 can differ.
- `ds.variants.load_counts(counts, *, sites=None, cell_selection=None)` returns a `VariantCounts` record: `.sites` (indexed by label such as `chrM:310`: `contig`, `position`, `name`, `channel`, `reference`, `depth`, `n_cells`), `.alleles`, `.cells` (indexed by `ids`: `listed`, `mean_depth`, `n_covered_sites`), `.depth` (`ids`, `site`, `depth`), `.counts` (`ids`, `site`, `allele`, `count`, plus `forward`, `reverse`, `quality_forward`, `quality_reverse`, `reads`, `support` where the source has them), `.calls` (`ids`, `site`, `allele`, `source`, `call`, plus `quality` where the source gives one, as Tapestri and DAb-seq do), `.contigs`, `.format`, `.count_unit`, `.depth_definition`. `sites` takes labels or positions (one contig), `cell_selection` a cell selection or IDs, as a list, NumPy array or pandas index; `cell_selection=[]` gives only the sites with their sums.
- `ds.plots.variant_depth(counts, group_by=None, groups=None)` draws each site's mean depth over named cells and, below it, the distribution of the cells' mean depth on log bins, one histogram per group in the group's colour; `group_by` takes one column or several and `groups` a label artifact, alone or together, grouped as the clone summaries group cells. Counts without depth at any site raise `ValueError`. Tables: `sites` and `cells`.
- `ds.variants.informative(counts, *, cell_selection=None, preset=None, min_cells=None, min_strand_correlation=None, min_vmr=None, min_depth=None, min_quality=None, mask=None, invalidate_cache=False)` scores every variant (alternative allele) that a chosen cell shows with mgatk's statistics and saves a `variant_selection` artifact. `preset="atac"`: `n_cells_conf_detected >= 5`, `strand_correlation >= 0.65`, `vmr > 0.01` (mtscATAC-seq). `preset="rna"`: `mean_coverage > 20`, `mean_quality > 30`, `vmr > 0.01`, no strand test (MAESTER). Every threshold is reached (`min_vmr=0.01` keeps a VMR of exactly 0.01), so the presets hold the next float above each strict value, such as `0.010000000000000002`, and keep exactly the published variants. A threshold given by name replaces the preset's; a test the counts cannot make (strands of cellsnp-lite counts, quality of four-column mgatk) raises `ValueError`. `cell_selection=ds.select_cells(counts, low=10)` scores nearly the cells mgatk scores, as the artifact entry above says. The VMR test of `"rna"` is broad: 470 of 5,664 variants on 50 MAESTER cells, so raise `min_vmr` for fewer.
- `mask`: `"numt"` (25 NUMT false positives, Laricchia et al. 2022), `"rna_editing"` (295, 2617, 13710, Bar-Yaacov et al. 2013), `"artifact_prone"` (301, 302, 310, 316, 3107, 16182). Masked variants are scored but never informative; masks need a 16,569-base rCRS contig (hg38 `chrM`, GRCh38 `MT`) and refuse hg19.
- Allele frequencies are undefined where a cell was not covered, never 0: the variance covers covered cells, as in mgatk. Signac sets them to 0 and stabilises the variance of low-depth cells, so its VMRs differ where coverage is sparse.
- `ds.variants.load_informative(informative)` returns a `VariantSelection`: `.variants` indexed by label (`chrM:310T>C`) with `site`, `position`, `reference`, `alternative`, `mean`, `variance`, `vmr`, `n_cells_conf_detected`, `strand_correlation`, `n_cells_over_5/10/20/95`, `max_heteroplasmy`, `mean_coverage`, `n_cells_covered`, `mean_quality`, `masked`, `informative`; plus `.counts`, `.cell_selection`, `.preset`, `.thresholds` and `.mask` (the settings as the artifact's provenance records them), `.n_cells`, `.stranded`, `.qualities`. `cytearc.variants.INFORMATIVE_PRESETS` holds each preset's thresholds, read-only.
- `ds.variants.frequency_clones(informative, *, n_neighbors=10, resolution=1.0, within=None, invalidate_cache=False)` calls clones from the counts that `informative` scored, as Signac's `FindClonotypes`: cosine similarity of square-root allele frequencies over the variants two cells both cover, cells that share a neighbour joined by the Jaccard index of their neighbourhoods as Seurat joins them (k includes the cell, joins below 1/15 dropped), Leiden. Every pair of cells is compared, so time grows with the square of the cells; `within` splits them. Tied cells (one informative variant each) join every other such cell, and a graph beyond `mem_budget` raises `MemoryError`. Cells without coverage, without any alternative read, or similar to no cell get no clone (diagnostics `cellsWithoutCoverage`, `cellsWithoutAlternative`, `cellsWithoutNeighbors`). Read depth does not count, so one stray read can put a cell in a clone, and a clone smaller than `n_neighbors` joins its most similar neighbour; the call warns when a group of scored cells is smaller than `n_neighbors`. `within="donor"`, or several columns such as `within=["donor", "sample"]`, keeps groups apart; every scored cell needs a label. Clones are `clone_1` (largest) on, numbered by size over every group, ties by their first cell. Saves a `clones` artifact that every clone summary reads.
- `ds.plots.variant_statistics(informative)` draws VMR against strand correlation (mean depth without strands), informative variants coloured and the rest muted grey, masked ones as crosses; `ds.plots.allele_frequencies(informative, clones=clones, max_cells=2000)` draws cells by informative variants, ordered by clone under a strip of clone colours with a legend of the clones, uncovered entries grey; the axis says "Cells (2,000 of 5,000)" when `max_cells` leaves cells out.
- `ds.variants.genotypes(counts, *, method, sites=None, alleles=None, min_umis=None, min_mutant_umis=None, min_fraction=None, min_posterior=None, swap_probabilities=None, min_quality=None, min_depth=None, invalidate_cache=False)` calls each cell's genotype at targeted sites and saves a cell-aligned `variant_genotypes` artifact (canonical `n_mutant_sites`, also `n_called_sites`). Calls: `wild_type`, `heterozygous`, `homozygous_alternative`, `alternative_present` (mutant, copies unknown, as GoT's UMIs give), `missing` (no evidence), `filtered` (evidence the rule did not call); each keeps the source's label. A cell not listed at a site is `missing`.
- `method="source"` adopts the tool's calls: GoT's `Genotype` (`MUT` is `alternative_present`), GoT-Multi's `genotype_final` before `genotype_merged` (`FalsePositive` is `filtered`), Gotcha's `genotype_pred` (`HET` is `heterozygous`), Tapestri's `NGT` and DAb-seq's `GT` (0 to 3: wild type, heterozygous, homozygous alternative, no call, which is `filtered` with reads and `missing` without; DAb-seq's `GT` of -1, one reference allele beside one not called, is no call too and keeps its label `"-1"`). A site with coordinates is genotyped once per alternative that holds calls, labelled like `chr1:115256513:G/A`; a named site (GoT target, FLT3-ITD) once. `min_quality` and `min_depth` filter adopted calls below a quality (`GQ`; a missing quality fails) or a depth at the site, as Tapestri's `gq_cutoff` and `dp_cutoff` do, and leave more cells ambiguous in genotype clones; counts of other formats, whose calls carry no quality, refuse `min_quality`, and a site where no cell holds depth (a GoT table of calls alone) refuses `min_depth`. `method="threshold"` refuses Tapestri counts, which hold no reference reads. `method="threshold"` applies GoT's rules as parameters: `min_mutant_umis=1` for patient samples (one mutant UMI calls `alternative_present`, none `wild_type`), `min_fraction` for cell lines (the allele's share must reach it, above 0.5 and up to 1; GoT's "more than 90%" is `min_fraction=float(np.nextafter(0.9, 1))`, while `0.9` also calls 9 of 10 UMIs); one of the two is required and `min_umis` defaults to 1. `method="gift"` applies GIFT's per-UMI model to giftwrap counts: gapfills weighed 0.01 per edit between the probe's wild type and mutant, each UMI's swap probability from `swap_probabilities` by reads (`{1: 0.5, 2: 0.2, 4: 0.05}`; reads are the entry's reads per UMI), uniform prior, called when the posterior reaches `min_posterior` (None, the default, is GIFT's rule, a posterior above 0.6, recorded as 0.6000000000000001) with `min_umis` UMIs of swap probability below 0.1; GIFT's published tables give the likelihood of no swap, so pass one minus it, and the table starts at 1 read. `alleles={"JAK2 c.1849G>T": ("CAC", "AAC")}` names a probe's wild type and mutant, in the probe's orientation, when the manifest does not; a wild type no cell shows raises. `method="gift"` refuses counts that are not giftwrap's.
- `ds.variants.load_genotypes(genotypes, *, sites=None)` returns a `VariantGenotypes`: `.sites` (`reference`, `alternative`, `source`, cells per call), `.cells` (`n_mutant_sites`, `n_called_sites`), `.calls` (`ids`, `site`, `call` ordered categorical, `label`, `reference_umis`, `alternative_umis`, three GIFT posteriors), `.counts`, `.method`, `.rules` (the method and rules as the artifact's provenance records them). `sites` takes labels, such as `"CALR"`, or positions on counts of one contig, such as `310`, which take every genotyped site there; `genotypes` and `genotype_clones` take them the same way.
- `ds.variants.genotype_clones(genotypes, *, sites=None, within=None, method="likelihood", dropout_probability=None, error_probability=None, min_size=None, min_posterior=None, invalidate_cache=False)` calls clones from each cell's mutant, wild-type and unknown (`missing`, `filtered`) calls, within one or several `within` columns. `"likelihood"` fits SCClone's mixture: each clone a set of mutations, each site's dropout and error probability estimated, the pooled error at most 0.1 (or held at every site by `dropout_probability`, `error_probability`), the number of clones by a greedy search on Schwarz's criterion; each cell takes its most probable clone (`clone_probability`), below `min_posterior` (0.6) it is `ambiguous`, clones under `min_size` (2) cells keep none, and wild-type cells take none. Each `within` group is fitted apart. A clone one site from its parent or from the wild type cannot be told from that site's dropout or error, so panels whose clones differ by one mutation (few GoT targets) use `method="exact"`: cells link when they agree at every site both were called at, and cells whose calls agree with two clones that disagree are left out as `ambiguous`. Labels `clone_1` and on by size. Saves a `clones` artifact; diagnostics `dropoutProbability`, `errorProbability` (the pooled rates the fit used), `cellsInSmallClones`, and `ds.artifacts.load(clones)` holds `site_labels`, `dropout_probability` and `error_probability` per group and site.
- `ds.plots.genotypes(genotypes, sites=None, group_by=None, groups=None, layout=None)` draws the fraction of each call per site and group (groups as `variant_depth`'s), and with `layout=umap_ref`, an embedding artifact whose cell selection is the genotypes' own, the cells at its coordinates as `ds.plots.embedding(layout=umap_ref)` draws them, coloured by their call at the one site that `sites` names, such as `sites=["CALR"]`; a layout of several sites or of other cells raises `ValueError`. Calls are named in words in the legend ("Wild type") and keep their tokens in the tables. Tables: `calls` (with `share`) and `cells`.

## Recipe

```python
cells = ds.snapshot_cell_selection("I")
counts = ds.variants.import_counts("mgatk/final", cell_selection=cells)
joined = ds.artifacts.inspect(counts).diagnostics
print(joined["unmatchedBarcodes"], "of", joined["fileBarcodes"], "barcodes name no cell")
ds.plots.variant_depth(counts, group_by="sample")
deep = ds.select_cells(counts, low=10)

# One variant's frequency per covered cell: count over depth.
loaded = ds.variants.load_counts(counts, sites=[3244], cell_selection=deep)
table = loaded.counts.merge(loaded.depth, on=["ids", "site"])
table["frequency"] = table["count"] / table["depth"]
```

```python
# Informative mitochondrial variants and the clones they mark (mtscATAC-seq).
deep = ds.select_cells(counts, low=10)
informative = ds.variants.informative(counts, cell_selection=deep, preset="atac", mask=["numt", "artifact_prone"])
selection = ds.variants.load_informative(informative)
selection.variants[selection.variants["informative"]].sort_values("vmr", ascending=False).head()
ds.plots.variant_statistics(informative)
clones = ds.variants.frequency_clones(informative)
ds.plots.allele_frequencies(informative, clones=clones)
ds.clones.load_clones(clones).clones.head()
```

```python
ds.clones.load_clones(clones).cells["ambiguous"].sum()

gift = ds.variants.import_counts("counts.1.filtered.h5", cell_selection=cells)

```

```python
# Tapestri: a store of its own, its passing variants and one extra, and the clones of NGT's calls.
reader = cytearc.TapestriReader("sample.dna+protein.h5")
cytearc.TapestriToZarr(reader, zarr_loc="sample.zarr").dump()
reader.close()
```

## Checks

- Check `unmatchedBarcodes` in `ds.artifacts.inspect(counts).diagnostics` first: a wrong prefix, suffix or pattern leaves every cell unlisted; `unselectedBarcodes` counts the barcodes of store cells outside the selection.
- Check `.contigs`: mgatk picks hg19's 16,571-bp sequence when the BAM's contig has that length, and nf-MiTo's `refAllele.txt` names no contig, which then never combines with a named one.
- Look at the depth plot before calling variants: most cells should cover most positions in mtscATAC-seq, while MAESTER and targeted panels cover few sites deeply.
- Frequencies need the depth: never treat a missing depth row as frequency 0.
- Check `ds.plots.variant_statistics` before trusting a preset: informative variants should sit apart, top right. Too many informative variants usually means noise passed the thresholds; raise `min_vmr` or add `mask`.
- Check the clones' diagnostics: many `cellsWithoutCoverage` or `cellsWithoutAlternative` cells mean shallow coverage or variants that mark few clones; such cells are not clones.
- Choose the threshold rule from the study design: one mutant UMI for patient cells, a 90% share for cell lines, whose many transcripts make PCR recombination likely. `alternative_present` makes no claim of zygosity; only GIFT and Gotcha's `HET` call heterozygotes.
- Check `.sites` of loaded genotypes: many `missing` or `filtered` cells mean few UMIs per cell, and genotype clones treat those sites as unknown, so a few covered sites give coarse clones.
- Check the sizes of genotype clones from targeted DNA: small clones beside the large ones are often doublets, which carry two clones' mutations, or cells that lack one mutation of their clone, which a subclone and that site's dropout explain equally. Hundreds of small clones under `method="exact"` mean allelic dropout splits them; use the default likelihood.
